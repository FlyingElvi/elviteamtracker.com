Your club's stats, posted in your Discord.

Elvi's Team Tracker follows your EA NHL Pro Clubs team and posts every game as it finishes — the real final score, the three stars, and who did what. Then it gives your players cards, head-to-head records and leaderboards to argue over.

What it does

Free, no ads, no premium tier.

  • Automatic box scoresEvery game your club plays posts itself in the channel you pick — final score, three stars, and the full skater and goalie lines.
  • Real scores, not segmentsWhen a private game lags out and gets replayed, the pieces are merged back into one true score instead of three fake ones.
  • No games against the CPUPractice and scrimmage games against the computer are never stored and never counted toward anyone's stats.
  • Player cardsPlayers link their gamertag once with /trackme, then /playercard gives them a card with their season on it.
  • Card statsFlip the card to its Stats face for the full season and the OVRs behind it, filtered to the format you actually play. Open on every server.
  • Head-to-head/versus knows your record against any player or club, the teammates you win the most with, and the ones who own you.
  • LeaderboardsSkater and goalie boards for your server's clubs, or for any club the bot has seen.
  • Weekly availability Coming soonA manager panel that collects who's in for which night, then builds the lineup and publishes it. Being finished now — it isn't open to every server yet.
  • Tracked by nameYou type the start of your club's name. Nobody has to go dig up an EA club id.

Setting it up

Two steps, about a minute. You need Manage Server on the Discord server.

  1. Add the bot Use the install link and pick your server. It installs to the server — there is no account install.
  2. Point it at your club Go to the channel where you want scores to land and run /trackprimaryclub name:<start of your club's name>. Type the start of the name — often your league's prefix, like LG. One match and it's tracked immediately; several and you pick yours from a menu. Already know your EA club id? Use clubid:<id> instead.

That's it. Games post in that channel from then on.

Options on /trackprimaryclub

OptionWhat it does
emoji:Shows a logo next to your club in scoreboards. Upload the emoji to your server first (Server Settings → Emoji) — a bot can't use emojis from servers it isn't in.
matches:all (default), or private_only if you only want league games posted.
backfill:0–5. How many already-played games to post right now. Default 0.

Don't see /trackprimaryclub? Three things, in this order:

  1. Do you have Manage Server? Every track command is admin-only, and Discord hides a command you can't run rather than greying it out. If /trackme shows up but /trackprimaryclub doesn't, this is why.
  2. Just added the bot? Commands can take up to an hour to appear on a new server. Ctrl+R (Cmd+R on Mac) in Discord usually forces it.
  3. Server Settings → Integrations → Elvi's Team Tracker → Commands. If somebody already restricted it there, that setting wins.

A few extras — challenges, milestone chases, streak records — are switched on server by server rather than shipped to everyone at once. If you want them on yours, say so in the support server.

How it works

The bot reads EA's public NHL Pro Clubs match data on a schedule — the same records the EA companion app shows. It stores the games your tracked clubs played, works out the real score and a per-match rating for each player, and posts the result. Nothing is scraped from your Discord, and the bot has no way to read your messages: it never requested the permission that would let it.

Where your data goes

Onto one self-hosted server, and nowhere else. It isn't sold, shared or used for ads, and there is no analytics or tracking anywhere — not on this site either. The Privacy Policy lists field by field what is stored and how to have it removed.
