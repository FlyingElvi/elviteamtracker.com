What's new

What shipped, and when. The log starts at the NHL 27 changeover in September 2026 — everything before that was a different game's data.

My clubs

November 12, 2026New
  • Every game of the clubs you've played for — not just the ones you were in. Pick a club and a lens (public 6s, 3s, private, league); any row opens the full game.
  • The club's numbers up front: record, win %, goals for and against, last 10, streak — and a scroll away, shots, power play and penalty kill, one-goal games, time on attack, faceoffs and more.
  • Opponents show their EA record next to their name, here and in your game log.

Scout any club

November 12, 2026New
  • Drew a club you've never heard of? In My clubs, Scout or track a club: type its name and see its record, its last games and who dresses — tracked or not.
  • Looking tracks nothing. Track it is there if you want the bot to follow that club from now on.

My player: more numbers

November 12, 2026Changed
  • More totals: power-play and game-winning goals, hat tricks, shot attempts, puck possession and ice time per game, breakaways, penalty shots, desperation saves… Three rows show, the rest is a scroll away.
  • Shutouts next to shutout periods, on your page and on the goalie boards.
  • The EA chip shows EA's own career numbers — every club, drop-ins included — with the full line as soon as EA lists you as a member of a club.

Easier to get around

November 12, 2026Changed
  • Your pages have their own addresses: /myplayer/ and /myclubs/. Old links still land there.
  • In the header, your pages (My player, My clubs) sit together, apart from the rest of the site.
  • On a phone, your clubs fit on one line you swipe; wide tables scroll on a thin line instead of a bulky scrollbar.

Every game, in detail

November 6, 2026New
  • A page for every game: both crests, the score, the three stars, and the full game sheet — every column of the score post, totals, TOA and power play, and the goalies.
  • On a phone, one team at a time (yours first) with the player's name pinned while the stats scroll. On a computer, both teams.
  • Open it from a row of your game log, from Last games on the home page, or from a score notification (View details).

Game score notifications, per club

November 6, 2026Changed
  • Type a club, then pick how much of it: All its games, League games only, or When not in lineup — the games you missed. Add as many clubs as you like.
  • A club the bot doesn't track yet starts being tracked when you pick it.
  • Got traded? Untick the club and it stops.

There's an app for that

November 5, 2026New
  • Elvi's Team Tracker installs on your phone like an app — no store, no download. On iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android: tap Get the app.
  • Your card and your player page, one tap away.
  • Notifications: in your menu, pick what buzzes your phone — duels, challenges, milestones, lobbies, game scores. On iPhone they need the installed app.

Your card, no server needed

November 4, 2026New
  • Any Discord account can sign in now — you don't need to be in a server that has the bot.
  • Link your gamertag on the home page and get your player card, your game log, your challenges and your clubs.
  • Signed in, the home page is yours: your card and your last games. Setting up the bot for a team moved to Add the bot.
  • Changed your mind? Remove my card in your menu unlinks you and takes the card down. Details in the privacy policy.

Sign in with Discord

October 18, 2026New
  • Sign in on this website with your Discord account — for people who already have the bot in one of their servers.
  • The leaderboards are now for signed-in members. The player cards stay open to everyone.
  • Signed in, the leaderboards open on your club with your row highlighted, and the Cards page lets you put your card on the web — or take it off — right there.
  • Every board of the bot is on the web now: your clubs one click away, and each league season under the League lens. Premium servers get their own boards there too. The Leaderboards button under the card now opens the site.
  • Discord only tells us who you are; nothing is posted, nothing else is read. Details in the privacy policy.

Leaderboards on the web

October 18, 2026New
  • Leaderboards for any club the bot tracks: every lens — all games, public 6s and 3s, private 6s, league — skaters and goalies, and every column sortable.
  • Live: a board is at most five minutes old. Share one by copying its link.

Player cards on the web

October 18, 2026New
  • A 🌐 Web button under your /playercard puts your card on elviteamtracker.com/playercards — the front, the stats and the last 10 games, drawn exactly like the one in Discord.
  • It's opt-in: the first press explains what goes public and asks. Only the players who said yes are on the page — and in its data.
  • The page is a snapshot, refreshed periodically. The card in Discord stays the up-to-date one.

Basic Server Setup

September 30, 2026New
  • Added to a new server, the bot now posts a welcome: what it does, the players' commands, and a Basic Server Setup button for the managers.
  • /elvisetup opens the same setup any time. Every club of the server lives there — your teams, affiliates and scouted rivals, each in its own channel, all games or private only — with the tracked list and Stop tracking under it.
  • /trackaffiliateclub, /trackscoutclub, /trackedclubs and /stoptracking are gone: it's all in the setup now. /trackprimaryclub stays.

Availability: the league calendar, and lineups players confirm

September 30, 2026New
  • A league manager panel: the league's game times, schedule and clock, and its seasons and weeks. A week off — a holiday, the All-Star break — keeps its dates but not its number, so the next one keeps counting.
  • The night pulse picks a week of the season, with each night's date on it. The pulse and the sign-ups each take a note for the players.
  • A published lineup asks its players: I'm in or Can't make it, and the answer shows beside each name.
  • The availability panel sorts its buttons into the week's and the team's, and keeps itself current — no Refresh button any more.
  • When the bot has a league's schedule, Post Week Availability puts the week up in one click: one post per night, with its game times and opponents. The games played off that schedule are recognized as league games on their own.

EA outages that weren't

September 22, 2026Fixed
  • EA's API looked down for seven hours. It wasn't — the bot's own HTTP/2 connection had gone stale and was never being retired. Games stopped posting for an afternoon; they were all picked up once the connection handling was fixed.

The week editor's Notes button

September 19, 2026Fixed
  • The Notes button on the week editor never responded. It does now.

Automatic MVP

September 18, 2026New
  • Every game now names an MVP from its star points, and goalies are eligible — a shutout can win it outright.

Season status, and the league-night flag

September 17, 2026New
  • A Season status button on the availability panel: where the club is in its campaign, without leaving the channel.
  • Two bugs in the league-night flag — one in the data, one in the interface — meant some games were filed under the wrong week. Both fixed, and the affected games were re-filed.

The Stats card opens to everyone

September 16, 2026New
  • The Stats face of the player card is now available on every server, not just the ones it launched on. Run /playercard and flip to it.

More than one lineup a night

September 15, 2026New
  • A night can hold several lineups. Back-to-backs and split sessions no longer overwrite each other.
  • Private games are no longer locked behind a separate setting.

/versus bff

September 14, 2026New
  • /versus bff — the teammates you actually win with. The inverse of /versus nemesis, and it finds the names for you.
  • A night's pulse can be skipped when the team already knows it's playing.

/leaderboard streaks

September 12, 2026New
  • Every streak record on one board, with where you sit on each of them.

NHL 27 launch day

September 4, 2026New
  • Adopting a club on the new game is a single gesture — no re-entering ids, no re-linking players.
  • Head-to-head records now span both games. Your record against somebody doesn't reset because EA shipped a sequel.
  • The player rating had a measurable bias against defencemen. It was measured, and corrected.

Availability 2.0

September 3, 2026New
  • The whole weekly flow, rebuilt around one persistent panel per team: the night pulse, the sign-up posts, the lineup editor, publishing, and the assignment that survives a restart.
  • Help buttons on both management panels, so nobody has to remember what a button does.

NHL 27 era in production

September 2, 2026Changed
  • EA's API switched to NHL 27 ahead of the game's release. The bot flipped with it, and NHL 26 history was kept rather than thrown away.

Card navigation, and nightly backups

August 17, 2026New
  • The player card moved to a select menu instead of a row of buttons — it stopped running out of room as faces were added.
  • A nightly backup of everything the bot stores, verified, kept for two weeks.
