Only the players who chose to share their card are here — press 🌐 Web under your own card in Discord to join them. The cards here can be a few minutes behind: the card in Discord is always the up-to-date one.

🌐 Put your card on the web?

Your player card will be published on ElviTeamTracker.com/playercards — a public page that anyone can open, search engines included.

What goes on it: your EA gamertag, your Discord avatar, your club and your card's stats (front, stats and last 10 games).
What doesn't: your Discord name, your servers, your Discord id.

It's not live. The page can be a few minutes behind — your card in Discord is always the up-to-date one.

Changed your mind later? Sign in here and remove it, or press ⚙️ on your card's Clubs face in Discord: it comes down at the next refresh, and no copy of it is kept on the site.
